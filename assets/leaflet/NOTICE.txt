Map tiles used by the GPS location picker (assets not bundled, fetched at
runtime — an internet connection is required to use the picker):
- Street: OpenStreetMap (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)
- Satellite / Satellite+Labels / Topographic: Esri World Imagery, Esri World
  Boundaries & Places, Esri World Topo Map (https://www.esri.com)
- Address search: Nominatim (https://nominatim.org/), OpenStreetMap data
