StarBox pushes this jar to Android devices/emulators via ADB and communicates
with it directly over its documented socket protocol for the multi-device
sync remote control feature (android-sync-manager.js). It is unmodified from
the upstream release. Do not replace with a different version without also
updating android-sync-manager.js — the wire protocol (launch arguments,
socket handshake, control message format) is specific to this exact version.
